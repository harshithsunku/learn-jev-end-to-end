# Regions and data residency

## Available regions
Acme Cloud runs in us-east, us-west, eu-central (Frankfurt), eu-west (Dublin), and ap-south (Mumbai).

## EU data residency
Workspaces pinned to eu-central or eu-west keep customer data, backups, and logs inside the European Union. Data residency is an Enterprise feature.

## Moving regions
Moving a workspace between regions requires a support ticket and a scheduled maintenance window.
