# Single sign-on (SSO)

## Supported protocols
Acme Cloud supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. SSO is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans.

## Enabling SAML
An organization owner opens Settings > Security > Single sign-on, chooses SAML 2.0, uploads the identity provider metadata XML, and maps the email attribute. After a successful test login, the owner can enforce SSO for all members.

## SCIM provisioning
Enterprise plans can automatically create and deactivate users through SCIM 2.0.
