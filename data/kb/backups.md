# Backups and restore

## Automated backups
Managed databases are backed up automatically every day. Automated backups are retained for 14 days on Pro and 35 days on Enterprise.

## Point-in-time recovery
Enterprise databases support point-in-time recovery to any second within the retention window. Pro databases can restore only to a daily snapshot.

## Manual snapshots
Manual snapshots are kept until you delete them and count toward storage billing.
