# Billing

## Invoices
Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and charged to the payment method on file.

## Disputes and refunds
Billing disputes must be opened within 60 days of the invoice date. Refunds for duplicate charges are processed within 5-10 business days.

## Plan changes
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
