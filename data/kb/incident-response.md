# Incident response

## Severity levels
SEV1 means a full outage or data loss; SEV2 means major degradation for many customers; SEV3 means minor impact or a single customer.

## Paging
SEV1 and SEV2 page the on-call engineer immediately. SEV3 creates a ticket for the next business day.

## Status page
The incident commander must post a status page update within 15 minutes of declaring a SEV1.
